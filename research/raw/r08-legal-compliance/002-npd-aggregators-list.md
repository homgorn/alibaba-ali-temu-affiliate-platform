### LEGAL BASIS
Per 422-ФЗ Article 4, operators of electronic platforms (операторы электронных площадок) are defined as platforms that:
- Provide search for work, orders, or buyers
- Transmit income information to FNS
- Withhold tax for payment (if authorized by taxpayer)

### IMPLICATION FOR AFFILIATE INCOME
Since major affiliate networks are NOT on this list, they do NOT automatically report affiliate income to ФНС. The self-employed taxpayer must:
1. Manually issue checks (чеки) in "Мой налог" app for each affiliate payout received
2. Self-report all income
3. No automatic withholding by the affiliate network
