## VERBATIM QUOTES (Original Language - English)

### Quick Answer
"You can buy from 1688 outside China in three ways: through a sourcing agent who pays suppliers in RMB and ships to you, directly with an international card where 1688's cross-border checkout appears, or via 1688's Cross-Border Pay for registered companies. Most overseas buyers start with an agent because 1688 has no built-in international shipping."

### Platform Reality
- "1688 is Alibaba Group's domestic wholesale marketplace — Chinese language, RMB pricing, and factory-direct prices typically 20–40% below the same supplier's Alibaba.com listing."
- "The platform doesn't ship internationally. Every overseas order needs a bridge: a sourcing agent, a freight forwarder address in China, or 1688's own cross-border program."
- "Agents charge roughly 3–10% commission depending on order size — usually far less than the price gap you save by skipping Alibaba.com."
- "Payment is the main barrier: standard checkout expects Alipay linked to a Chinese bank. International card checkout and Cross-Border Pay (跨境宝) close that gap for some accounts."
- "Plan for consolidation: 1688 suppliers ship domestically to a warehouse first; your agent or forwarder then combines parcels into one international shipment."

### Four Realistic Routes

| Route | Who pays the supplier | Typical cost on top | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sourcing agent | Agent (you pay agent by PayPal/card/wire) | ~3–10% commission + QC/handling | First-time buyers, mixed orders from several suppliers | Agent receives, inspects, consolidates, ships DDP/air/sea |
| Direct — international card | You, at 1688's cross-border checkout | Card/FX fees | Buyers whose account shows the international payment option | Availability varies by account and region; still need a forwarder for shipping |
| Cross-Border Pay (跨境宝) | Your registered company | Program fees | Companies registered outside China | Requires business documents and Alipay compliance review — takes days |
| Just use Alibaba.com | You, normal checkout | 20–40% higher unit prices | Small trial orders, buyers who value English support and Trade Assurance | Simplest path; cashback of up to 9% partially offsets the higher price |

### Rule of Thumb
"A useful rule of thumb: if your order is under a few hundred dollars or you're testing a supplier, the Alibaba.com premium is often worth paying. Once you reorder the same SKU at volume, the 1688 route usually wins even after agent fees — the 3–10% commission bands are small next to a 20–40% price gap."

### Agent Route Step-by-Step
1. "Find the product. Browse 1688.com with Chrome's built-in translation, or image-search with a product photo (the camera icon in the search bar) — it's often more reliable than translated keywords."
2. "Check the price tiers and MOQ. 1688 listings show unit prices that drop at quantity breakpoints (e.g. 2+, 50+, 500+ pieces). Confirm the tier you'd actually hit."
3. "Pick an agent. Established 1688 agents publish fee schedules — commission, QC, repackaging, storage. Compare at least two; transparent pricing is the main trust signal."
4. "Send links, get a quote. The agent confirms stock and price with the supplier in Chinese, then quotes you: goods + commission + domestic shipping + estimated international freight."
5. "Pay the agent. Typically PayPal, card, or bank transfer. The agent pays the supplier in RMB through 1688's normal checkout."
6. "Consolidate at the agent's warehouse. Suppliers ship domestically (often 2–5 days). The agent inspects, photographs, and combines multiple parcels into one shipment."
7. "Choose international shipping. Express (5–10 days), air freight, or sea freight for bulk; DDP options exist to most major markets. You pay actual weight/volume after consolidation."

### Payment Methods Detail
- "Standard checkout expects Alipay linked to a mainland Chinese bank card. Without one, this path is closed."
- "International card checkout — some accounts accessing 1688 from abroad see an option to pay with Visa/Mastercard at checkout. When it appears it's the simplest direct route, but it isn't shown to every account or region, so treat it as a bonus rather than a plan."
- "Cross-Border Pay (跨境宝) is 1688's official program for overseas companies: you submit company registration documents to Alipay's compliance team, and after verification you can pay suppliers directly, as described in payment guides. Built for registered businesses, not individuals."

### Shipping Reality
"Even when payment works, remember the second half of the problem: the supplier still ships to a Chinese address. Direct payers pair checkout with a freight forwarder who provides a warehouse address and handles the international leg."

### EU Customs Note (Critical for 2026)
"Since 1 July 2026 the EU has applied a temporary flat customs duty of €3 per item on consignments worth up to €150, replacing the previous duty exemption. The charge is assessed per item by tariff classification rather than per parcel, so a single consolidated shipment holding several distinct product types is charged several times over. The European Commission's own worked example puts one silk blouse and two wool blouses at €6, because they fall under two different tariff sub-headings."

"That detail matters more for 1688 than for most platforms, because consolidation through an agent is the normal route and a consolidated box is exactly where distinct tariff classifications accumulate."

### Can I Buy Without Agent?
"Yes, if two conditions hold: you can pay (your account shows international card checkout, or your company is verified for Cross-Border Pay) and you have a Chinese delivery address from a freight forwarder. If either is missing, an agent is the practical route."

### Is 1688 Legit?
"1688 is owned and operated by Alibaba Group, so the platform itself is legitimate. Risk sits at the supplier level — quality variance and no English dispute flow — which is why agents bundle inspection and photos before international shipping."

### Cashback Note
"Activation is manual. 1688 cashback isn't switched on automatically for every account — you must contact support to enable it."
"Click before you pay. As on the other platforms, the cashback button must be clicked before submitting payment — eligible items show their status in the cart. This matters most if you pay direct; if an agent pays on your behalf through their own account, the order won't be tagged to you."
