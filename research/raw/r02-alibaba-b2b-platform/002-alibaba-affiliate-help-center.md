## VERBATIM QUOTES (Original Language)

### Commission Structure
"As an affiliate partner, you will receive a commission (excludes any insurance fees, delivery fees or other relevant fees and expenses) for every qualified order you bring to alibaba.com. The commission rate will increase based on performance for those publishers who are able to deliver large volumes of quality traffic."

### Alibaba.com CPS Affiliate Program - Commission Structure (Updated July 1st, 2026 PT)

| Buyer Classification | Order Price Range | Commission |
|---|---|---|
| New Buyer | Order Price < $5,000 | 8% of order price |
| New Buyer | $5,000 ≤ Order Price ≤ $10,000 | Fixed of $300 per order |
| New Buyer | Order Price > $10,000 | Fixed of $400 per order |
| Existing Buyer | Order Price < $5,000 | 3% of order price |
| Existing Buyer | $5,000 ≤ Order Price ≤ $10,000 | Fixed of $100 per order |
| Existing Buyer | Order Price > $10,000 | Fixed of $200 per order |
| Extra Bonus for New Buyer's First Order in Core Countries | $500 and above | +$20 per order |

"*This is not a permanent commission rule. If you need any clarification on the updated commission rules, please feel free to email ads.alibaba@service.alibaba.com"

### Qualified Order Status
- "Order Status must be 'Trade Completed'. It means we will only process the commission to our affiliate partner once the order status changes to 'TradeCompleted'. This indicates that the buyer has acknowledged receipt of the products and there have been no alterations or reimbursement logistics."
- "Spam status is 'N'."
- "The number of days between the Order Completed Date and the Order Paid Date must be less than 180 days. Once an order has a completed date, the commission payout will be processed for the corresponding month. This order will be settled in the Monthly Bill report."
- "Order prices do not include any shipping costs, taxes, or marketing discounts; the Est. Commission in the CPS report is a preliminary commission calculated according to the commission rules and is not the final settlement commission. The final settlement commission will be based on the Monthly Bill."
