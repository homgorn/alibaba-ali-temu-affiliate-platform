### How to Become a Channel Partner (3 Steps)
1. **Apply**: "Submit your company profile and business capabilities through the partner application process."
2. **Get Authorized**: "Complete training and certification to represent Alibaba.com in your market."
3. **Start Serving**: "Onboard sellers, deliver services, and grow your business with ongoing partner support and incentives."

### Key Distinction from Affiliate Program
This is a **Channel Partner Program** for agencies/service providers who help **sellers** (suppliers) onboard and succeed on Alibaba.com - NOT an affiliate program for driving buyer traffic. Partners earn "performance-based rewards through partner incentive programs" by servicing sellers, not by referring buyers.
