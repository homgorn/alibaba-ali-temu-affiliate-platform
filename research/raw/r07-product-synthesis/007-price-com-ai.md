## Platform Overview

Price.com AI is a conversational shopping assistant built on top of Price.com's price comparison engine.

### Interface
- Chat-based: "Need shopping advice? Ask Price.com AI and unlock bigger savings."
- "Ask for a product. Get every store, every price and every coupon compared, with the cash back already worked in."
- Suggested prompts: "Kids' Halloween costumes", "Halloween candy in bulk", "Outdoor Halloween decorations", "Early Black Friday deals", "Holiday gifts under $50", "Warm fall jackets under $100", "Cozy throw blankets", "Space heaters for small rooms", "Game day tailgate gear", "Cordless leaf blowers"

### Example Interaction (from page)
> User: "Adidas Samba men's size 12"
> AI: "Samba OG · 12 stores compared" → shows adidas (2% cb, $100), Zappos (2% cb, $100), eBay refurbished ($80)

### Capabilities
- Natural language product queries
- Multi-store price comparison in chat
- Cashback integrated into results
- Coupon codes applied automatically

### Limitations
- Reactive only (user must ask)
- No proactive monitoring/alerts
- No price history
- No landed cost calculation
- No counterfeit detection
- No specification verification
- No cross-border analysis
- Requires account creation to use fully
- No API for developers
