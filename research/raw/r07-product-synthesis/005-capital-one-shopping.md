# Source: Capital One Shopping (formerly Wikibuy) - Coupon/Cashback Browser Extension

**Content Type:** Consumer-facing cashback/coupon platform

## Platform Overview

Capital One Shopping is a browser extension + web platform that:
- Automatically finds and applies coupon codes at checkout
- Offers cashback rewards at partner merchants
- Shows price drop alerts for viewed/purchased products
- **Includes Temu** (up to 13.5% back for new customers, 3% existing)

### Key Features
1. **Auto-coupon application** - Tests codes at checkout
2. **Cashback rewards** - Percentage back on purchases
3. **Price drop alerts** - Notifies when tracked products drop
4. **Product search** - Compare prices across retailers
5. **Browser extension** - Works automatically on merchant sites

### Merchant Coverage (observed)
- Sam's Club (+2%), Ulta (+4%), Walmart (+2%), DoorDash (+$16), Macy's (+8.5%), Dick's (+4%), Verizon (+$87.50), Target (+2%), LEGO (+5%), Walgreens (+5%), **Temu (+13.5% new / 3% existing)**, Disney+, Xfinity, Paramount+, Home Depot, CVS, UGG, Adidas (+14%), Nike (+9%), PetSmart, North Face (+12%), Michael Kors, Sephora (+5%)

### Temu Specifics
> "Not eligible for orders with delivery address outside of the US or to U.S. territories. Not eligible for orders placed using the TEMU app, orders that originate from clicking on a TEMU email, orders..."

### Rewards Redemption
- Gift cards, PayPal, bank transfer
- "Capital One Shopping earns a commission when you make eligible purchases from merchants and we may share those commissions with you as rewards"

### Limitations
- US-focused (merchants listed are US retailers)
- No price history charts
- No landed cost calculation
- No counterfeit detection
- No specification verification
- No cross-border analysis (AliExpress, Alibaba not listed)
- Coupon testing only at checkout (not pre-purchase decision support)
- Cashback model = affiliate commission sharing
