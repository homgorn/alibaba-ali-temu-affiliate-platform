# Glossary

> **Summary:** Domain vocabulary for this project. Affiliate and Russian tax
> terms are defined once here so wiki pages can use them without re-explaining.
> Terms marked ⚠️ carry a project-specific caveat that is easy to get wrong.

---

## Affiliate & commerce

**Affiliate / partner program** — a programme where a third party is paid a share
of revenue generated by their traffic. The publisher (us) links; the merchant
(network/retailer) pays.

**CPS / CPA / CPI** — payment models. **CPS** = per sale (what we use). **CPA** =
per action (any tracked conversion). **CPI** = per app install.

**EPC** — earnings per click. The single most important number in this project.
`EPC = revenue_per_click × conversion_rate`. **Currently unknown** — see
[UNIT-ECONOMICS](../50-strategy-traffic/UNIT-ECONOMICS.md).

**TAC** — traffic acquisition cost, `CPC / conversion_rate`. Paid traffic is only
viable when `TAC < EPC`. This is the test that decides paid vs organic.

**Cookie window / attribution window** — how long after a click a sale still
counts. ⚠️ **AliExpress is 3 days** — unusually short, and the binding strategic
constraint on this project.

⚠️ **Deferred commission** — many networks do not pay on order placement. A
commission may be held until the order reaches a "completed" state, then clawed
back on refund. Alibaba.com B2B requires `TradeCompleted` **and** requires the
paid→completed gap to be under 180 days.

**Feed** — a bulk export or API surface of product data. Feeds, not ad-hoc
scraping, are what this project's engine ingests.

**MMP** — mobile measurement partner (AppsFlyer, Adjust). ⚠️ **Alibaba.com B2B
explicitly refuses all MMP integration** and uses only its own attribution. This
is why B2B cannot use a Keitaro-class tracker.

**Attribution model** — how a sale is credited to a referrer. Last-click is most
common; multi-touch variants exist.

**Hot Products** — a platform-designated category of high-commission items.
⚠️ On AliExpress this is where commission reaches up to 90% while the nominal
baseline is 0–9%. **Category selection, not traffic tactics, drives margins.**

## Technical

**Module / plugin contract** — the single interface every network integration
implements. Because the engine treats CSV and live API as two sources behind one
interface (D-014), a network with **no API** — such as Temu — costs a config
change rather than an architectural fork.

**Idempotent upsert** — writing the same record twice leaves the same state as
writing it once. Mandatory for ingestion: feeds are re-fetched and re-run.

**Watermark sync** — incremental sync that remembers the last successful cursor,
so a re-run fetches only what changed rather than the whole catalogue.

**Product identity / dedup** — deciding that two listings from different sellers
are the same product. ⚠️ The core data problem. See PRODUCT-IDENTITY (pending R4).

**GTIN / EAN / MPN / ASIN** — product identifiers. ⚠️ GTINs are often absent or
faked on marketplace listings; Amazon's ASIN is Amazon-only and means nothing for
an AliExpress listing.

**Landed cost** — the total a buyer actually pays: item price + shipping +
customs/duty + VAT + delivery time. ⚠️ **The highest-value gap we can fill** —
almost no tool computes this for cross-border purchases, and it is the question
buyers actually have.

**HTML-first** — every page ships complete, meaningful content in the server
response. No content may exist only after JavaScript executes. Basis of D-010.

**`.md` twin** — a Markdown version of a page at a predictable URL, per the
llms.txt v2 spec, so an LLM can retrieve it cheaply.

**llms.txt** — a curated Markdown index at a site root listing LLM-friendly
pages. Lighthouse now audits for it. Distinct from `robots.txt` (what may be
accessed) and `sitemap.xml` (all indexable URLs).

**GEO** — Generative Engine Optimization: being cited by AI answer engines.
Distinct from SEO (ranking in search results).

**Deferred/scraping risk — `.md` twin URL convention** — either
`page.html.md` (append) or `page.md` (replace extension). This project picks one
**consistently**; mixing both would fragment retrieval.

## AI crawlers

**OAI-SearchBot** — OpenAI's crawler for **ChatGPT search**. Sites that block it
"will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers". Allow it.

**GPTBot** — OpenAI's crawler for **model training**. Separate control from
OAI-SearchBot. Blocking it affects training, not search visibility.

**OAI-AdsBot** — validates landing pages submitted as ChatGPT ads.

**ChatGPT-User** — fetches a page when a *user* asks about it. ⚠️ **Robots.txt
rules may not apply** to it, because the fetch is user-initiated.

**robots.txt propagation** — ⚠️ changes take **~24 hours** to take effect. Do not
debug distribution in the same minute you edit.

**JS rendering by AI crawlers** — ⚠️ **UNVERIFIED and treated as NO.** OpenAI's
documentation does not state whether OAI-SearchBot executes JavaScript. The
project assumes it does not, because designing for "no JS" is safe under every
hypothesis while designing for "JS is rendered" breaks catastrophically if it is
not. See [LLM-DISCOVERABILITY](../50-strategy-traffic/LLM-DISCOVERABILITY.md).

## Russian tax & law

⚠️ **Scope note:** the audience is global/English-first (D-021), so these terms
are background context rather than a planning constraint.

**Физлицо** — physical person, no business registration.

**Самозанятый / НПД** — self-employed status under the special "tax on
professional income" regime. Low rate, income ceiling, cannot hire.

**ИП** — individual entrepreneur. Higher ceiling, can hire, more accounting
obligations. **This is the operator's status.**

**УСН** — simplified taxation system, the usual regime for ИП and ООО. Variants:
"доходы" (revenue) and "доходы минус расходы" (profit).

**ПСН** — patent taxation system, available to certain ИП categories.

**НДС** — value-added tax. Thresholds and rates change; verify current-year
figures against nalog.gov.ru rather than assuming.

**54-ФЗ** — the Advertising Law. Governs marking advertising and marking
sponsored content, including on social platforms.

**38-ФЗ** — Consumer Protection Law. Governs claims about product quality and
prohibits misleading advertising.

**152-ФЗ** — Personal Data Law. Consent requirements and **database localisation**
obligations for personal data of Russian citizens.

## Project-specific shorthand

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **D-NNN** | A decision in `docs/logs/DECISION_LOG.md`. Never renumbered or reused. |
| **R-NNN** | A risk in `docs/logs/RISK_LOG.md`. (Three digits — distinct from research tracks `R1`–`R8`, which are always unhyphenated.) |
| **F-NNN** | A feature in `feature_list.json`. `passes: false` until a verifier proves it. |
| **G-NN** | A research gap. Open tasks are first-class results, not noise. |
| **M-N** | A roadmap omission, tracked in `ROADMAP.md`. |
| **FR-/NFR-/AC-/EC-/OS-** | Spec constructs: Functional / Non-Functional Requirement, Acceptance Criterion, Edge Case, Out of Scope. |
| **L0–L7** | Test ladder levels (see `ROADMAP.md` Phase 3). **L7** = the SEO/GEO guard. |
